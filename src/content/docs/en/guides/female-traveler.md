# Female Traveler's Survival Guide

> The general era guides (e.g. the [[xia-dynasty|Xia Dynasty]]) assume a neutral or male body.
> This guide covers the **incremental risks**: in a society with no written law and no
> enforcement, your safety is determined by **deterrence + a patronage network**, not by
> rights. All universal strategies (skills, knowledge advantages) still apply.

## 1. Risk Map

| Risk | Likelihood | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sexual violence | **Peak in the first days** | Alone, no shared language, claimed by no one — the first weeks are the danger maximum |
| Forced marriage / allocation as a resource | High | In tribal societies an unattached woman is treated as an unowned resource — giftable between clans, claimable as spoils |
| Pregnancy and childbirth | Lethal tier | A leading cause of female death in premodern eras: no C-section, no antibiotics, no transfusion |
| Labor exploitation via strength disadvantage | Medium | Without a protected status: worked hardest, fed last |

## 2. The First 30 Days: Camouflage and Movement

- **De-gender your appearance**: bind the chest, cut or wrap the hair, gray the face with
  ash and dirt. The goal is not "passing as a man" but **"unclassified"** — what is not
  noticed and not discussed does not become a target.
- **Feigned illness**: display an on-and-off skin complaint or cough; rumors of
  "contagion" repel most harassment. **Do not overdo it** — being taken for a plague-bearer
  gets you expelled. "Fluctuating symptoms" is the calibrated sweet spot.
- **Movement iron rules**: travel by day, in company, along inhabited river valleys;
  never move at night; sleep beside your patron or in the middle of the women's group.

## 3. Patronage Network and Identity Construction

- **First patron: a respected elder woman** — the chief's mother or wife, the midwife,
  the priestess. The path in: your modern midwifery knowledge (handwashing, clean cord
  cutting, postpartum care) is the door-opener. **Volunteer as the midwife's assistant** —
  the fastest route to the power center.
- **Identity: the "midwife + healer" double role.** In most early societies female
  spirit-mediums (witches, shamans) genuinely exist and are feared — the identity's taboo
  is itself a protective barrier. Make "touching her brings divine punishment" village
  consensus (reinforced by a few small "fulfillments").
- **Clerical status and marriage**: in some societies shamans do not marry or are "wed to
  the god," exempting them from allocation — but this depends on local custom.
  **Observe first, then bet**; never assume.

## 4. Physiology Management (Zero Modern Supplies)

### Menstruation

Dried moss, dried grass, or washed hemp cloth strips (boiled and sun-dried after each use);
change daily and clean with boiled-and-cooled water to prevent infection — poor menstrual
hygiene in a world without antibiotics can escalate into a fatal one.

### Contraception

**Herbal contraception in antiquity is unreliable and dangerous — do not gamble on it.**
The only reliable strategy is "the act never happens," which is also a core reason for the
early-period security strategy. If marriage becomes unavoidable:

- Cycle method (avoiding ovulation days): limited reliability, better than nothing;
- Extended lactational amenorrhea: highly effective for the first ~6 months postpartum —
  historically the main birth-spacing mechanism.

### If pregnancy is confirmed

Immediately raise "prenatal self-monitoring + birth preparation + postpartum infection
prevention" to top priority — the one time your own modern knowledge most directly
saves your own life.

## 5. Crisis Response

- **A forced marriage is proposed**: never refuse to the face (humiliating the proposer
  is lethal). Defer to "divine will" — "the spirit must be asked; an auspicious month must
  be chosen" — to buy time for your patron to intervene.
- **Violence has already occurred**: seek your patron and clan adjudication, not private
  revenge. Remember: your scarce skills are the long-term bargaining chip —
  **early survival outranks justice**; live first, into the day you have standing.
- **Ally with other women**: the midwife–wet-nurse–elder-women network is the *actually
  functioning* power structure of early societies. Your modern knowledge (midwifery,
  gynecological hygiene) makes this network need you naturally.
