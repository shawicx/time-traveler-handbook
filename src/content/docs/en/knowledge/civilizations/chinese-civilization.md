## Overview

**Chinese Civilization** is one of the most common destinations for time travelers,
covering the long span from the Xia (c. 2070 BC) to the Qing (1912 AD). It is not a
single state but a family of dynasties sharing a script (Chinese characters), core
philosophies (Confucian / Daoist / Legalist), bureaucratic tradition, and an agrarian
economic base.

If you are traveling to East Asia, you will almost always land inside a specific dynasty —
so identifying *which* dynasty you are in is the first priority. The gap between dynasties
is enormous: **Tang openness and Qing isolation are two different worlds**.

## Reminders for Travelers

1. **Language**: Old Chinese (pre-Qin) is not mutually intelligible with Modern Chinese.
   Ming-Qing Mandarin is barely recognizable.
2. **Script**: Traditional / seal / clerical scripts change by era; simplified characters
   appear only after the 20th century.
3. **Currency**: Cash coins (round with a square hole) circulate long-term, but minting
   quality varies; silver does not circulate widely until Ming-Qing.
