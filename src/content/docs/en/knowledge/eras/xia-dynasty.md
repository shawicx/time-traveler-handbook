## Overview

The **Xia Dynasty** is the first legendary hereditary dynasty of Chinese civilization,
lasting roughly from 2070 BC to 1600 BC. Tradition holds that it was founded by Yu the
Great, with a capital near Yangcheng (modern Dengfeng, Henan). It sits at the transition
from the Neolithic to the Bronze Age, its political structure evolving from a loose tribal
alliance toward early kingship.

## What You Must Know Before You Arrive

### What You Will See

- **Early bronze**: A few small bronze tools and ritual vessels, but stone and bone tools
  remain the backbone of production.
- **Jade**: High-grade jades (gui tablets, zhang scepters) are central symbols of noble
  status and ritual.
- **Palace-city**: The Erlitou site shows large palace foundations, but far smaller than
  later imperial capitals.
- **Writing**: **No mature writing system.** Records rely on knot-tying and carved marks;
  travelers cannot depend on written information.

### Social Structure

| Tier | Role |
| --- | --- |
| King | Yu and his descendants; military and ritual leader |
| Nobles | Heads of clans, holding local power |
| Commoners | Farmers, hunters, potters |
| Slaves | Mostly war captives; the lowest status |

### Economy & Technology

- **Agriculture**: Millet is the northern staple, harvested with stone sickles and bone
  spades; rice appears in the south.
- **Pottery**: Grey and black wares are refined; wheel-throwing is widespread.
- **Bronze**: Limited to small items; casting is in its infancy; iron does not exist.
- **Currency**: **No metal money.** Trade is by barter or cowrie shells (sea / bone).

## Survival Essentials

1. **Disease is the greatest threat**: There is no medicine; a minor infected wound can
   kill. Bring antibiotics.
2. **Diet**: Millet porridge, wild greens, a little meat. Cooking is boiling in pottery
   tripods — no stir-frying (the iron wok arrives only in the Song).
3. **Status**: You are either noble, commoner, or slave — there is no "free traveler."
   Attach yourself to a clan quickly.
4. **Beliefs**: Animism and ancestor worship are central. Profaning a ritual may be fatal.
